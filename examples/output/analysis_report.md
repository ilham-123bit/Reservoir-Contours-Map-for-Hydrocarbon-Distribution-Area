# Synthetic structural-contour analysis report

## Dataset and quality

Input: `data/synthetic/synthetic_surface.csv`. This is a fictional synthetic depth surface generated by this project. The grid has 101 x-nodes by 91 y-nodes, spacing 100.000 m (x) and 100.000 m (y), 97.99% valid nodes, and 97.64% triangulated mesh coverage. Missing depth nodes remain excluded from contour regions; mesh triangles with any missing vertex are omitted.

## Structural interpretation at the selected threshold

The depth convention is larger numerical value = shallower structure (the synthetic depths are negative metres). The raw-grid crest is at (4700.0, 4700.0) m with depth -2261.590 m. The selected threshold is -2321.590 m, giving raw-crest relief 60.000 m. The computed region area is 19319808.9 m² (19.319809 km²); status: **closed**.

The reported grid-exit saddle is (600.0, 7200.0) m at smoothed depth -2344.559 m. It is the bottleneck node on the maximum-bottleneck 8-neighbor route from the maximum of the normalized smoothed surface to the model-domain edge. This smoothed crest may differ from the raw-grid crest reported above and used to identify the threshold closure component. The saddle is domain dependent and is not an independently interpreted geologic spill point. The selected threshold contour and grid-exit saddle are distinct quantities.

## Threshold sensitivity

Tested 6 thresholds. Across scenarios classified as closed, area spans 7.425–23.082 km². Rows marked `boundary_limited` reach the model boundary and should not be interpreted as a fully enclosed closure. See `spill_sensitivity.csv`, `closure_area_sensitivity.png`, `structural_relief_sensitivity.png`, and `closure_scenarios.png`.

## Controlled robustness experiments

- Smoothing: closure area ranges 19.241–19.330 km² across the tested scenarios.
- Missing Data: closure area ranges 16.765–19.694 km² across the tested scenarios.
- Resolution: closure area ranges 21.223–21.239 km² across the tested scenarios. The resolution experiment uses a zero-noise surface to isolate sampling. Missingness and smoothing experiments use fixed seeds; missing-data coverage changes because triangles touching null nodes are not integrated. See `robustness_scenarios.csv` and `robustness_comparison.png`.

## Assumptions and limitations

The surface is synthetic and has no real reservoir meaning. Crest depth is the maximum valid raw-grid value. Threshold regions use normalized Gaussian smoothing and 8-neighbor node connectivity. Area is integrated over a fixed-diagonal piecewise-linear triangular mesh by clipping each valid triangle at the selected threshold; it is an approximation to a continuous surface and is limited by grid resolution and missing cells. Boundary-touching regions are flagged. The digital grid-exit saddle depends on model extent, sampling and smoothing. Smoothing can shift or suppress local highs; the raw crest is retained for reporting. Neither a closure-shaped high nor any calculated metric proves hydrocarbon charge, seal, reservoir quality or accumulation.
